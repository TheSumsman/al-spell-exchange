Settle it today. ALPG p.3: “You may copy spells from a character's spellbook immediately after a session in which you both played.” For this Epic the whole event counts as one session, so any wizard here may copy from any other wizard here — but you must record it now.

Downtime is the real limit. You earn 10 DT per session, and levelling up costs 10 DT. Gold is rarely what stops you. Order of Scribes wizards copy ten level 1–4 spells for 1 DT.
